# Convention philosophy

*Last updated: 2026-09-12*

> How WoW2 chooses its own conventions from its philosophy, knowledge and engineering evidence.

## Authority

- must choose conventions for WoW2's own goals and philosophy.
- must treat external conventions as inputs to evaluate, not rules inherited by authority.
- may combine, adapt or reject conventions from Microsoft, other ecosystems, books and blogs.
- must apply an adopted WoW2 rule until its owner explicitly revises it.
- must distinguish an external recommendation from an actual language, runtime or protocol constraint.
- must account for verified platform behavior when evaluating a house rule.

Rationale → [why WoW2 owns its conventions](philosophy-rationale.md).

---

## Evaluation

- must compare concrete benefits, failure modes and mitigations against the intended usage.
- must state when a disadvantage depends on a particular usage pattern or framework feature.
- must distinguish an intrinsic limitation from a limitation shared by the proposed alternative.
- must support technical incompatibility claims with the relevant contract, source or a reproducer.
- must not substitute an authority's preference for an explanation of the tradeoff.
- must preserve intentional house choices when a sweep finds only an external stylistic disagreement.
