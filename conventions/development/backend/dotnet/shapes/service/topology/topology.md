# Topology

*Last updated: 2026-09-10*

> Recognized service deployment-topology vector; topology-specific rules are not yet selected.

## Activation

- must declare process ownership, deployment units and cross-process contracts when adding another deployable process.
- must document data ownership and failure boundaries when splitting a monolith or adding cross-service coordination.
- must use the current product's declared topology until that change is active.
- must not infer a microservice or modular-monolith mandate from this shell.

---

## Open

- topology-specific rules remain unwritten until a product makes that deployment choice.
