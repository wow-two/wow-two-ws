# Existing WoW2 ideas: commercial reassessment

Reviewed 2026-09-28. Local idea specifications establish intended scope, not shipped behavior, customer adoption or revenue. This review did not run the applications or inspect their independent repositories. Existing engineering can lower incremental build cost; it cannot establish market fit.

## Recommendations

| Existing idea | Recommendation | Commercial issue | Smallest useful test |
|---|---|---|---|
| [ForeverPin](../forever-pin-spec.md) | Retain as a focused experiment | Editable QR destinations and branded links already have strong free and paid competitors. “Codes never die” creates ongoing hosting obligations. | Pick one buyer with a recurring routing or destination-health problem. Compare their current workflow against a $9–19/month proposed add-on; do not sell permanence without funded maintenance. |
| [TranscriptForge](../transcript-forge-spec.md) | Narrow to creator-owned or authorized source material | Broad transcription/repurposing competes with substantial bundled products. Arbitrary YouTube caption retrieval is a permissions dependency. | Test correction consistency or caption delivery QA with supplied files; require three paid repeat users before channel-scale ingestion. W0510/W0511 are related research candidates. |
| [PoseCoach](../pose-coach-spec.md) | Bounded differentiation test; later revenue wave | Capture latency, device permissions, model costs and native camera substitutes complicate a cheap global launch. The existing September competitor report already records several rivals. | Test a single repeated capture outcome and measured user preference. Price API usage only after real latency/cost measurements. |
| [FlowDeck](../flowdeck-spec.md) | Reframe around an explicit debugging job | Generic .NET request visualization faces Aspire's OpenTelemetry logs, traces and metrics. A new dashboard alone is weak differentiation. | Demonstrate a source-linked diagnosis that a developer cannot obtain as quickly from their present tools. Charge only after repeat use on real incidents. |
| [Wheelhouse](../wheelhouse-spec.md) | Keep as internal portfolio infrastructure | The specification describes an internal control plane. It is not independent evidence of an external buyer. | Measure operator time saved across the first few products; do not count internal usage as subscription revenue. |
| [Activity Matcher](../activity-matcher-spec.md) | Defer from global-first portfolio | Venue freshness, local discovery and maintaining city supply create operational work; a directory alone has weak repeat payment. | One city and a clear paying operator first, only if this local direction becomes intentional. |
| [Route Vibe Player](../route-vibe-player-spec.md) | Defer pending platform feasibility | Spotify restricts important streaming commercial uses, content ingestion into AI, and overlapping/mixed playback. | Map every feature to current platform permissions before building or proposing pricing. A lawful non-streaming companion would need a separate value proposition. |
| [Gaussian splatting](../gaussian-splatting-spec.md) | Defer generic scanner/reconstruction platform | Current Polycam offers Gaussian splats and team workflows. Reconstruction compute, storage and media support can exceed the lean budget. | A supplied-file quality or handoff tool for one professional workflow; no new capture hardware or hosted reconstruction commitment. |
| [Interactive 3D web experiences](../interactive-3d-web-experiences-spec.md) | Treat as a capability or service until productized | A delivery technique does not specify a repeat buyer or subscription job. | Identify a repeat publishing workflow with three paying clients before extracting a SaaS. |
| Haven / ListingShelf | Separate internal utility from possible external workflow | Workspace registry describes channels/CRM and source adapters; crawler access and local marketplaces reduce portability. Runtime and customer state were not verified. | Test a user-supplied listing workflow independent of unauthorized scraping; do not assume a global data feed exists. |
| Ocharo Studio / Brand Workspace | Existing domain access may enable testing | Workspace registry names active products; fashion rendering, physical operations and broad editing platforms differ from cheap global micro-SaaS. | Isolate a software-only approval, rights, localization or asset-handoff job using voluntarily supplied files. Domain familiarity is not a commercial validation result. |
| [Privacy/compliance generators](../micro-saas-deep-search.md) | Defer broad legal output; retain narrow evidence administration | Legal maintenance, jurisdiction variation and free generators make generic “cheap to run” claims incomplete. | A record collection or expiry workflow with an explicit boundary; no legal sufficiency guarantee. |
| [Receipt-to-CSV](../micro-saas-deep-search.md) | Test only a specific import destination | OCR is a component, not a durable distribution advantage; accuracy, personal data and exceptions create support costs. | A known template and owner-supplied samples; measure correction time, willingness to pay and extraction costs. |
| [Accessibility checker](../micro-saas-deep-search.md) | Prefer regression/evidence workflow | A scan cannot establish complete accessibility compliance. Free scanners and existing developer tools limit standalone scan pricing. | Repeated triage and remediation evidence for an agency; keep manual review and scope visible. |
| [Open Graph image / cron / webhook tools](../micro-saas-deep-search.md) | Require a narrow buyer-specific failure mode | Generic utilities face free tiers and established infrastructure vendors; reliability promises create ongoing operations. | Validate a repeated customer-specific job such as sanitized replay or missing supplier callbacks before building another general tool. |
| [AI garden / virtual try-on](../passive-income-automation-spec.md) | Later revenue-funded experiments | Inference cost, image fidelity and episodic demand weaken low-cost subscription assumptions. | Prepriced bounded credits on supplied images, with measured quality and repeat purchase; no unlimited plans. |
| [Automated ambient/music channels](../passive-income-automation-spec.md) | Exclude from this SaaS shortlist | Content monetization depends on platform distribution and rights; projected ad revenue is not SaaS MRR. | Keep in a separate content business analysis if pursued. Do not repeat unverified health or therapeutic claims. |
| [City vision](../city-vision.md) | Exclude | Physical infrastructure and geographic operations violate this research's scope. | None for this software-only portfolio. |

## Fresh evidence that changes older assumptions

- [Short.io pricing](https://short.io/pricing/) advertises substantial free branded-link functionality and paid plans; editable QR/link infrastructure is already offered. Exact feature and billing combinations need checkout verification.
- [YouTube caption download documentation](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/captions/download) requires appropriate permission to edit the video. Public viewing alone does not authorize the official caption-download API.
- [Spotify Developer Policy](https://developer.spotify.com/policy) restricts AI ingestion of Spotify content, overlapping/mixed playback, and commercial streaming applications. This is a platform feasibility constraint, not a conclusion about every possible companion app.
- [Aspire dashboard documentation](https://aspire.dev/dashboard/overview/) describes logs, traces and metrics, including standalone OTLP use. Generic observability is an established substitute for FlowDeck's broad framing.
- [Polycam pricing](https://poly.cam/pricing) now lists Gaussian splats, export options and business collaboration. Older low-price and missing-collaboration assumptions should not drive investment. The inspected page displayed Basic $150/year or $30/month and Business $400/user/year; plans and included features require current checkout confirmation.
- [The existing PoseCoach competitor analysis](../ai-photo-director-market-analysis.md) already records competing camera/posing products. Its exact prices are historical September 10 observations, not freshly reverified in this exercise.

## How to use the older research

Keep [micro-SaaS deep search](../micro-saas-deep-search.md), [market analysis](../market-analysis.md) and [passive-income automation](../passive-income-automation-spec.md) as hypothesis history. Their market-size labels, near-zero cost assumptions, one-week build estimates and high margins are not measured traction. A time-box or landing-page visit threshold can govern an experiment, but does not independently validate demand.

The new catalogue separates observed commercial offers, analyst scores, proposed prices, and future paid-pilot gates. Existing products should compete for the same scarce distribution and support capacity as new ideas.
