# Review iterations and changed decisions

All passes occurred during the 2026-09-28 research session. This is an analytical audit, not an experiment log of customer behavior.

## Passes completed

1. Read existing WoW2 specifications and prior market searches; separate intended products from evidence of adoption.
2. Generate and score 250 hypotheses in each of four lanes. Related variants receive family labels. Physical operations and broad platform clones are explicitly rejected.
3. Research 40 chosen leads per lane while generation continues. Record current competitor features, prices and free substitutes.
4. Construct the reproducible 300-row comparison cohort: 160 researched plus 140 other high-scoring screens. This is a purposive review cohort, not a retroactively claimed sequential market-validation funnel.
5. Falsify early favorites against exact incumbent features; update source records and lane decisions.
6. Review the cross-lane selection independently. Remove duplicate numeric risk penalties, expose reserve decisions, correct alternative pricing, merge related caption workflows and exclude rejected exploratory ideas.
7. Retain 90 evidence-ranked and 10 exploratory candidates, with one explicit caption-family preference. Model costs, customer counts, churn, acquisition and support; recommend three bounded paid-workflow comparisons.

## Central-review corrections

- Operational risks remain written comments. They no longer subtract another score on top of the same risks already present in lane dimensions.
- Subscription-versus-one-time alternatives render as “or,” not additive charges. Only subscription buyers count in MRR arithmetic.
- Lane reserve labels and original reasons are displayed in both detailed Markdown and the browser. A retained reserve is a discovery option, not an approved build.
- Caption QA and subtitle revision are treated as a correlated family. W0511 is explicitly preferred for a smaller, more falsifiable test.
- W0758 fragrance blind rating and W0787 packing reset were rejected and removed from exploratory positions. Local support-fixture scrubbing and rare-book history replace them.
- All 100 selected rows have individual live competitor references; the other 840 unsourced screens are labeled honestly.

## Remaining empirical work

There are no completed interviews, paid pilots, acquisition tests or retention cohorts. Public problem reports for retainers and documentation are narrow, self-reported and often historical. Competitor marketing, an existing free workflow, and a specific public complaint are different evidence types; none should be upgraded to customer validation for WoW2.
