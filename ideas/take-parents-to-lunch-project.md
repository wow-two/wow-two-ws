- The page reads the day the other way round from its name: parents eat lunch with their child in the school
  cafeteria, to see school food and talk to staff.
- The page gives 2 different founders: the history credits the "Kiwi" family magazine, the FAQ credits chef Ann Cooper.
- The page links related days, e.g. Take Your Webmaster to Lunch Day; National Today runs a page per observance.
