## What it is

A large circular city — the Core, 1.2 M — ringed at 18 km by six smaller circular cities of 200 K
each, every settlement self-sustaining. Multi-layer ground: utilities, private traffic, freight and
transit each get their own level so the surface belongs to people. At the Core's centre sits the
Culture Ring — 20 cultural sectors, car-free, each showing its culture's present, its pasts, and its
crimes alongside its achievements.
